What we keep

When you message us on WhatsApp at +44 7776 010007, we receive your WhatsApp name and number, your messages and anything you send with them, and delivery and read receipts. We keep our replies too.

Why

To reply to you, and to keep a record of our correspondence with you.

How

WhatsApp, operated by Meta, delivers your messages to us. We store them in our own records system, hosted by Cloudflare and Neon. We may use AI services to help draft replies; a person reviews every reply before it is sent.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the service providers above, so they can provide their services to us.

How long

For as long as we need it for our correspondence with you and our legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us at info@liao.info to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Meta's own handling of WhatsApp data is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.
